01 · My Hypothesis

The End Is The Beginning

This is a speculative idea connecting black holes, singularities, the Big Bang and the expansion of the universe. It is not an established scientific theory.

We don't know what a black hole is ultimately made of. If a body falls inside a black hole, its matter and energy continue into a region where classical general relativity predicts extreme spacetime curvature.

In the classical picture, matter falling into an idealized black hole can reach a singularity. A singularity, however, should not simply be imagined as an ordinary physical point made of infinitely compressed particles. It signals that our classical theory has reached a limit.

My hypothesis asks whether the extreme interior of a black hole could somehow be connected to the beginning of an expanding universe. In this picture, what appears to us as the end of matter inside a black hole could represent a transition into a new cosmic phase.

I also explore whether the physics behind cosmic expansion could be connected to this transition. This is an open question: current cosmology does not establish that dark energy is dark matter's energy, nor that dark matter surrounds a black-hole singularity in the way proposed here.

“What if the end of a black hole could somehow become the beginning of a universe?”
02 · Established Physics

What We Know

The hypothesis starts from real physics, but the connection between these facts is speculative.

BLACK HOLES

Extreme Gravity

A black hole is a region of spacetime from which, once inside the event horizon, signals cannot escape to the outside universe through ordinary future-directed paths.

GENERAL RELATIVITY

Singularities

Classical general relativity predicts singular behavior in idealized black-hole solutions. This is one reason physicists expect a deeper theory that combines gravity with quantum physics.

COSMOLOGY

The Expanding Universe

Observations show that the universe is expanding. The standard cosmological model describes this history using an early hot, dense state and includes dark matter and dark energy as distinct components.

HAWKING RADIATION

Black-Hole Evaporation

Quantum-field calculations in curved spacetime predict that black holes can emit Hawking radiation and lose mass over extremely long timescales. The exact endpoint requires physics we do not yet have experimentally confirmed.

03 · Falsifiability

What Could Prove It Wrong?

Scientific principle: A hypothesis must be open to evidence against it. The goal is not to prove this idea right, but to find out whether nature agrees with it.

1. A complete theory of quantum gravity could rule out the transition

If a successful theory of quantum gravity showed that black-hole interiors cannot transition into an expanding spacetime, this hypothesis would be strongly challenged.

2. Observations could be incompatible with a black-hole origin

Future measurements of the cosmic microwave background, large-scale structure, gravitational waves, or the geometry of the universe could reveal features inconsistent with a black-hole origin.

3. Black-hole evaporation could end without a new universe

If the final theory of black-hole evaporation showed that evaporation simply removes the black hole without any transition to a new expanding spacetime, the proposed connection would fail.

4. Dark matter and dark energy may have no connection to the proposed mechanism

Current cosmology treats dark matter and dark energy as different phenomena. If future experiments establish their properties in a way that provides no physical connection to the proposed transition, this part of the hypothesis would need to be abandoned or changed.

5. The hypothesis needs a unique, testable prediction

The most important challenge is to identify an observable effect predicted by this hypothesis that standard cosmology does not predict. Scientists could then search for that effect. If it were not observed, the hypothesis could be falsified or would require major modification.

The key question: What observable feature should exist if our universe was born inside a black hole, but should not exist if the standard cosmological model is correct?
